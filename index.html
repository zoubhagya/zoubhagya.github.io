About

I am an Assistant Professor of Economics at HSE University in Moscow. I run field and lab experiments to study how information, identity and social norms shape economic decisions in developing countries, with most of my work set in India.

I received my PhD in Economics from the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur in 2023, with a thesis titled Essays in Experimental Economics. Before joining HSE, I was a Visiting Assistant Professor at the Centre for Research on the Economics of Climate, Food, Energy and Environment (CECFEE), Indian Statistical Institute, Delhi, where I remain a Junior Fellow. I am also an International Associate of the Environment for Development (EfD) network at the University of Gothenburg.

Earlier, I completed an MA in Economics at Jawaharlal Nehru University and a BTech in Mining Engineering at NIT Rourkela.
